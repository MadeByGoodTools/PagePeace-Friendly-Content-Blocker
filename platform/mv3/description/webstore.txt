PagePeace is a friendly Manifest V3 content blocker for Chrome, Edge, Brave,
and other Chromium browsers.

Its default protection combines uBlock Origin's maintained filters with
EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Peter Lowe's ad and tracking list, badware protection,
and the Online Malicious URL Blocklist. Optional privacy, annoyance, mobile,
and regional rulesets are available from the filtering dashboard.

PagePeace 1.1 adds the latest upstream MV3 filtering improvements, including
more reliable cosmetic filtering, improved procedural filters, stronger
scriptlet support, better imported-list performance, custom filter backup and
restore fixes, and safer handling of non-website browser pages.

Simple mode offers clear Standard, Strong, and Maximum protection levels.
Advanced mode exposes filter lists, custom filters, matched rules, the element
picker, and site-specific controls. Filtering remains local to the browser.

PagePeace is a GPLv3 fork based on uBlock Origin and uBlock Origin Lite. It is
not an official uBlock Origin release and is not endorsed by the upstream
project.
